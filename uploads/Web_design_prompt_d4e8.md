# 3. DESIGN LANGUAGE & LAYOUT SYSTEM

## Design system philosophy

Use a **structured enterprise design language** with strong grids, precise alignment, generous whitespace, sharp contrast, and clear information hierarchy.

Make the UI visually powerful through composition—not decoration.

### Layout standards

- Use a consistent 12-column desktop grid.
- Center content inside a responsive maximum-width container, approximately 1280–1360px.
- Maintain consistent horizontal margins.
- Use an 8px-based spacing system.
- Give sections generous but controlled vertical spacing.
- Align headings, cards, screenshots, and CTAs precisely.
- Use modular content blocks rather than arbitrary floating elements.
- Create visible hierarchy without overcrowding.

### Cards and panels

Prefer structured rectangular cards with sharp edges or restrained 6–12px corner radii.

Avoid excessively rounded, pill-shaped content cards.

Use subtle borders and minimal shadows.

No excessive glassmorphism, floating neon panels, heavy blur, or decorative clutter.

### Backgrounds

Use:

- Solid Deep Green or Dark Accent.
- Controlled gradients between the two official greens.
- White and Soft Neutral structured surfaces.
- Very subtle spotlight lighting.
- Ultra-light document-grid or AI-inspired connecting-line accents.

Background elements should always remain subordinate to the content.

### Imagery

Prefer real VIDUR product screenshots, professional interface mockups, authentic legal books, research results, document analysis visuals, and approved professional media.

Product interfaces must appear crisp and realistic.

Use high-quality mockups only when they accurately represent existing functionality.

No cartoon characters, decorative 3D mascots, futuristic robots, or generic stock imagery.

# 4. THE INTERACTION PHILOSOPHY

Make VIDUR dynamic and engaging through **intelligent, restrained motion**.

Motion must communicate hierarchy, reveal information, explain workflows, or provide interaction feedback.

Do not animate merely to make the website look busy.

### Approved interactions

**Scroll-reveal animation**

Headings and content cards enter with subtle opacity and vertical movement.

Suggested movement: 8–16px.

Suggested duration: 400–600ms.

**Card hover interaction**

Use subtle elevation, border highlighting, or a 2–4px movement.

Suggested duration: 160–240ms.

**Button interactions**

Introduce refined hover color changes, subtle arrow movement, and clear pressed/focus states.

Avoid dramatic button scaling.

**Workflow transitions**

Use subtle connecting-line animations, progressive step reveals, and controlled state changes.

**Interactive tabs and comparisons**

Use smooth content transitions and clear selected states.

**Navigation animation**

Use restrained dropdown transitions and active navigation indicators.

**Video and product previews**

Use interactive media only where it adds useful product context.

### Do not use

- Bouncing cards or text.
- Magnetic buttons that move unpredictably.
- Animated floating blobs.
- Cursor-following effects.
- Excessive parallax.
- Neon borders.
- Continuous unnecessary motion.
- Flashing or rotating decorations.
- Large zoom-in entrance animations.
- Background particles or animated noise.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide an equally usable experience without animation.

The goal is to make the site feel **precise, responsive, polished, and expensive**.
